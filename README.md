# Alegebra-test (I need to edit out the geometry part)
Engineers are some of the most important jobs that exist nowadays. If it hadn't been by them, humanity wouldn't be were it is right now, from the technology at the reach of our hands, to life-saving machinery and structure. Engineers have relevance anywhere in the world, despite their specialization they have direct impact on human lives, making critical their general knnnowledge to ensure the quality and safety of their actions. 

In this context, the fundamental bases of engineering are algebra and geometry, despite of the specialization. Every good engineer must be familiarized with these topics and at leats have a basic knowledge of them. 

This test is designed to measure the knowledge of the users in these topics, the program first asks the user which topic would they like to test first, either algebra or gemoetry, then it generates 5 random questions based on the topic chosen and analyzes the answers given by the user to the machine calculated. At the end it asigns a grade to the user, allows them to save the results in a text file and recomends didactic material based on the answers given.
