# Alegebra-test
Engineers are some of the most important jobs that exist nowadays. If it hadn't been by them, humanity wouldn't be were it is right now, from the technology at the reach of our hands, to life-saving machinery and structure. Engineers have relevance anywhere in the world, despite their specialization they have direct impact on human lives, making critical their general knnnowledge to ensure the quality and safety of their actions. 

In this context, the most important basis for engineering is algebra, since this topic is required for the expertise and full comprehension in any of the engineering areas. Thus making crucial de practice and understanding of algebra for a succesful engineering career. 

This test is designed to evaluate the users knowledge on different topics of algebra. At the beginning of the program, the user is asked if they want to make a general test, having only a few questions per topic, or do a deep dive in a specific topic. The topics included are first and second degree equations, systems of equations of 2 and 3 variables, development of binomials, faactorization of equations and simplification of algebraic fractions. 

At the end of the test, the user is asked if they want to consult a didactic resource to reinforce their knowledge based on the questions that they got wrong, and the program should throw them the material in accordance to the mistakes made. 

# Instructions 
Inputs: 
--Topic to be tested, either one of the above or general test
--Answers given by the user throughout the test
